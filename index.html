We are BUSS!

The Bioengineering Undergraduate Student Society (BUSS) is the student council that supports bioengineering students in their academic, technical, social and professional growth.

What we do

From study sessions to industry nights, we are here to make the bioengineering experience better for everyone.

Academic

Study help, course advice and peer support throughout the year.

Technical

Workshops and design projects that turn classroom ideas into real builds.

Social

Events that bring students together, from game nights to end-of-term parties.

Professional

Career panels, alumni connections and industry partnerships.
